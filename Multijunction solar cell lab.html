About these numbers

How accurate is this model?

Real physics: photons are absorbed only above a bandgap, the excess becomes heat, junctions are in series so current must match, voltage rises with concentration, and the bandgaps and layer order follow real III–V stacks.

Simplified: a 5778 K blackbody replaces the AM1.5G or AM0 spectrum; every photon above a bandgap is absorbed; each junction loses a fixed 0.42 V and the fill factor is fixed at 0.86; there is no temperature, series resistance, radiative recombination or lattice-mismatch damage. Layer thicknesses and photon speeds in 3D are not to scale.

How close: the presets give about 24% (1J GaAs), 32% (3J), 37% (3J inverted metamorphic), 46% (4J at 665 suns) and 42% (6J). The reported records are 47.6% (4J, 665 suns) and 39.5% (6J, 1 sun), so the model is good for trends and can overshoot a little.

Where the spectrum goes

Solid colour is energy turned into usable voltage. Pale colour above it is lost as heat. Grey is below the lowest bandgap and passes through.

Current from each junction

mA/cm² per sun. Each bar is the photocurrent in that junction’s own band. The black marker is the current the whole series stack can carry. With thinning on, top cells pass their surplus down; with it off, the shortest bar limits the stack.
